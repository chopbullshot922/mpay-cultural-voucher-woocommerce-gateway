# Referral Program - 15% Commission

## How it works

Refer a new customer who purchases a commercial license for this plugin. You earn 15% of the license sale price.

## Who can participate

Anyone.

## Commission

- 15% of the license sale price
- Paid within 30 days of customer payment
- Minimum payout: 50 EUR
- Bank transfer (SEPA/international) or PayPal
- Applies to license sales only

## How to claim

1. Fill out CLAIM-FORM.md
2. Send to incontact@terabitlab.com before or when the customer contacts us
3. Customer must mention your name in first email, or introduce both parties in same thread

## Rules

- Submit before or simultaneously with customer first contact
- Retroactive claims not eligible
- Self-referrals not eligible
- One commission per customer
- Terms may change with 30 days notice for future referrals
